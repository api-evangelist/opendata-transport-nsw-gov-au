This post explains the more than 10 patronage datasets available on the Open Data Hub, including Opal Patronage, Opal Tap On/Off, Opal Trips by mode, and Train Station data. It notes that data goes through privacy de-identification before publication, which affects release timing, and helps readers pick the right dataset for their analysis.

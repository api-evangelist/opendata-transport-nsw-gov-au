Transport for NSW's Car Park API offers real-time and historical occupancy data for Park&Ride and Sydney Metro facilities across Greater Sydney, powering apps like ParkNPay, AnyTrip, and Live Traffic NSW, while also helping urban planners optimize traffic flows and assess infrastructure needs.
